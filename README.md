# Punch Power — Support & Privacy

Remember the old arcade punching machines — the coin-op ones at the bar, the boardwalk, the bowling alley, with the big pad you'd wind up and wallop to light up a score? Then you'd hand it to your friends and argue over who hit hardest. We loved those. So we rebuilt one and shrank it onto your wrist.

The world's tiniest arcade — on your wrist. … No coins, no pad, no line — just raise your fists and settle who's strongest.

**Punch Power** is a free arcade toy for Apple Watch. Throw a punch in the air and get a score from 0–1000, or switch to Time Attack and jab as fast as you can for 10 seconds. It's just for fun — **not** a fitness or punch-strength measuring device.

## How to play
- Open the app on your Apple Watch.
- **Punch Power:** tap **PUNCH**, brace through the 3-2-1 countdown, and throw one hard straight punch into the air. The watch scores it 0–1000.
- **Time Attack:** tap **TIME ATTACK** and jab as fast as you can (either hand) for 10 seconds.
- Tap your belt on the home screen to see your Career stats and belts.

## ⚠️ Safety
- **Clear your space** — no people, pets, walls, lamps, or ceiling fans within swinging range.
- **Punch the air only.** Never hit real objects.
- **Don't overswing** — keep it loose; don't yank your shoulder or elbow.
- Wear the watch band **snug** so it stays put.
- This is a toy for entertainment only. Play at your own risk and don't hurt yourself.

## Privacy Policy
_Last updated: September 22, 2026_

Punch Power does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes **no network connections** of any kind.

Everything the app saves — your high scores, career stats, and a one-time motion calibration — stays on your Apple Watch and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes that data.

Motion data from your watch is used only, on-device, in the moment, to score your punch, and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Punch Power uses a **workout session** while you play. This keeps the watch screen awake during your punch, and lets the app show your **heart rate** as a fun "how hard you worked" readout in Time Attack. Your heart rate is shown on-device only — it is **never saved to the Health app, never stored off your watch, and never transmitted**.
